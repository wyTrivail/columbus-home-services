10Economics

Get paid when the job is done — not when the lead is sent.

Phase 1 · Pilot

Free or near-free qualified jobs. We are buying trust and liquidity, not revenue.

Phase 2 · Transaction

Once we own booking, payment, and completion, we take a share of jobs that actually finish.

One completed job · illustrative

Plumber keeps ~$308
$42
Homeowner pays $350 NewsBreak take rate — 12% launch hypothesis, not a price

Variable costs come out of that $42: payments, support, refund reserve, incentives, AI. Contribution margin gets measured in the pilot — never assumed.

Plumbers should judge us on cost per completed job — not cost per lead.
